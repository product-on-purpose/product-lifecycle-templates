# product-lifecycle-templates v0.27.0

Release date: 2026-11-22. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). This is a minor
release with two new bundles. Nothing was removed or renamed, so nothing you use today breaks.

## Summary

**You can now write down your team's design principles and its product principles.** Each is a short standing
set of beliefs, agreed once and used to settle trade-offs. Design principles settle decisions about the
experience of a product. Product principles settle what to build, for whom, and what to refuse. They are the
library's 57th and 58th bundles.

## New

- **`design-principles`, in one lean size.** Each principle carries what it rules out, a question a reviewer
  asks, what it rests on and a decision it settled. The template also asks which principle wins when two
  conflict, who owns the set, and what event prompts a review.
- **`product-principles`, in one lean size.** Each principle carries a statement, why the team holds it, the
  trade-off it accepts, what it rules out and a decision it settled. The guide keeps it apart from design
  principles, which govern the experience rather than the product's direction.
- **Both join `standing-standards` as `foundation` members**, the family's ninth and tenth, by
  [ADR 0089](../internal/decisions/0089-design-principles-joins-standing-standards-as-a-foundation.md) and
  [ADR 0090](../internal/decisions/0090-product-principles-joins-standing-standards-as-a-foundation.md). The
  catalog gains entries 213 and 214.
- **Competitive analysis is admitted and specced, but not built.**
  [ADR 0091](../internal/decisions/0091-competitive-analysis-joins-strategy-docs-as-an-eighth-member.md) admits
  it to `strategy-docs`, and its build is issue #361.

## Improved

- **Both skills now recognize a request for design or product principles.** An agent decides whether to load a
  skill from its description, so a type missing from it may never reach the skill. No existing trigger was
  removed. Two sentences that are not triggers were shortened to make room under the length cap.
- **The product strategy one-pager points at the new bundle.** Its Product Principles guidance now says to keep
  a standing set in `product-principles` and to name its most-used rules in the one-pager (`product-strategy`
  0.1.1).
- **The build costs are published.** Design principles cost $11.60 and product principles $13.85 at API list
  rates, each for 17 agents on Sonnet 5.5, in [`bundle-builds/INDEX.md`](../../bundle-builds/INDEX.md).

## Fixed

- **Both principles guides could pass a set with nothing behind it.** A set with no owner, no owned review
  trigger and no rule for conflicts still cleared the rubric on its total. A 0 on any of those three rows now
  fails the set, and an honest "N/A" with a reason scores 1. Both bundles move to 0.1.1.
- **Five claims that no source said something were wrong.** The bundles' own cached sources say each one, and
  each is now credited to its source.
- **The positioning statement and the North Star metric gave stale places in the strategy chain.** Dated notes
  now place them fourth and fifth among the built members.

## Known issues

- **Nothing here makes any bundle better.** All 58 are `beta`, and no page calls one proven.
- **Nothing read shows that keeping a principles set improves decisions.** The product principles guide says its
  research found no measured effect of a set on decisions, and no named case of a set used to decline a request
  from outside the team.
- **The efficacy evidence still supports no quality claim, in either direction.** Of five runs, four were void.
  The latest one's below-zero result sits on a measure the protocol does not gate on, and that measure was chosen
  for what the templates lack, which biases it against them.
- **A search filtered to one axis value can be cut short.** The MCP server's `search_templates` returns at most 8
  candidates, and `utility` holds 12 bundles, `deliver` 10, `foundation` 10 and `develop` 9. `total_matched` in
  the response tells you when a list was cut.
