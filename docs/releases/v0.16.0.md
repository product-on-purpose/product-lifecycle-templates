# product-lifecycle-templates v0.16.0

Release date: 2026-10-11. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Summary

**The library grows from 38 bundles to 42 with four new templates: an experiment design doc, an experiment
readout, a tracking plan and a problem statement.** The design doc and the readout plan and report one
experiment, and together they form `experimentation-docs`, the tenth family. The tracking plan lists which
events a product records, and why. The problem statement frames one problem before anyone names a solution.
It is the first bundle on the `define` phase, so every lifecycle phase now holds at least one bundle.

None of the four is proven to help anyone. Each is researched, and each says where its sources disagree.
A decision record now says what the `define` phase is for: framing the problem. The PRD and the rest of the
delivery chain stay on `deliver`.

## New

- **[`experiment-design-doc`](../../templates/experiment-design-doc/)** - the plan for one experiment, fixed
  before the test runs. Lean states the hypothesis, who is tested and how they are split, the primary and
  guardrail metrics, the smallest effect the test is sized to detect, and the rule that will decide the
  result. Full adds a tracking note and the validity checks committed to in advance. It draws on product
  experimentation vendors, academic preregistration and UK public-sector evaluation.
- **[`experiment-readout`](../../templates/experiment-readout/)** - what one experiment found, and the ship,
  iterate or stop decision the results drove, measured against the plan the design doc fixed. Lean carries
  the results with their uncertainty, the validity checks, the decision and its owner. Full adds the
  deep-dive and the further analysis. Its example reports a null result and says what the null rules out
  and what it does not.
- **[`tracking-plan`](../../templates/tracking-plan/)** - the standing, product-wide plan of which events and
  properties a product records, and why. Lean holds the naming rules, the event and property catalog and
  who owns it. Full adds privacy and retention, and how a change to the plan is made. One feature's slice of
  the plan stays in that feature's PRD.
- **[`problem-statement`](../../templates/problem-statement/)** - who has a problem, what it costs them and
  the evidence that it is real, written before anyone names a solution. Lean ends with the decision the
  statement informs. Full adds how people cope today, what is not part of the problem, how the team will
  know it is solved, and the open questions.
- **The [`experimentation-docs`](../internal/contracts/experimentation-docs.md) family contract**, the
  tenth, and the first on the `measure` phase. It pairs a plan with its report, as `test-plan` and
  `test-summary-report` do for testing.

## Improved

- **Every quotation in the four new research logs was checked against the source's raw text**, not a
  retrieval tool's summary.
- **The business case now names the problem statement as the document that comes before it.** Its guide
  gains a row for it, and its example says so in a dated note.
- **Two catalog entries were corrected.** The tracking plan gains "data tracking plan" as an alias and drops
  two names no source used. The experiment readout's note now reads "ship, iterate or stop decision", and
  says that wording is this library's position.
- **A change that breaks the website can no longer merge.** The site build is now a second required check
  on `main`, beside the content gate, so a site that fails to build can no longer sit behind a green
  content gate.

## Fixed

- **Three pages undercounted the template variants the MCP server can fetch.** The README, STATE and the
  install guide said 71 from the 35th bundle onward, while the server already served 79. They now say 85,
  which is what the server serves.
- **STATE said "All nine" family contracts** after a tenth had been adopted. It now names all ten.
- **The experiment design doc's example said the readout's example was not yet built.** It now links
  that example, with a dated note.
- **The build workflow offered a research tier word the research-log contract rejects.** It offered
  `standard`, and now offers `standards`, which the contract accepts.

## Known issues

- **Nothing here makes any bundle better.** All 42 are `beta`, and no page calls one proven.
- **The problem statement was researched without McKinsey's problem statement worksheet.** Eight attempts
  across two research passes timed out, so no McKinsey field name appears in the bundle, and its research
  log says so.
- **One search can still not list a whole phase.** The MCP server's `search_templates` returns at most 8
  candidates, and the `deliver` phase holds 9. `total_matched` in the response tells you when a list was cut.
- **Build cost is measured, not a rate.** At API list rates, the experiment design doc cost $13.35, the
  experiment readout $12.96, the tracking plan $14.88 and the problem statement $19.97
  ([`bundle-builds/`](../../bundle-builds/INDEX.md)). The problem statement's figure includes a three-agent
  research pass after drafting.
