# product-lifecycle-templates v0.17.0

Release date: 2026-10-15. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Summary

**The library grows from 42 bundles to 44 with two new templates: a North Star metrics document and a
business continuity plan.** The North Star document names the one metric that shows customers get the value
a product strategy promises, and the input metrics a team can move. The business continuity plan keeps an
organization's critical business processes going through a significant disruption. Specs for three more
types are now written and accepted: a trade study, a use case specification and a data dictionary. None of
the three is built in this release.

Neither new bundle is proven to help anyone. Each is researched, and each says where its sources disagree.

## New

- **[`north-star-metrics`](../../templates/north-star-metrics/)** - one product's North Star metric, the case
  for choosing it, and the input metrics a team can move. Lean carries the metric, why it was chosen, its input
  metrics, what it serves, and when the team will revisit it. Full adds the candidates considered, guardrails,
  what the metric does not tell you, and a definition history. The formula, data source and targets stay in
  `kpi-dashboard`. It sits third in the `strategy-docs` chain, between the product strategy and the roadmap.
- **[`business-continuity-plan`](../../templates/business-continuity-plan/)** - the standing plan for keeping
  critical business processes running through a disruption. Lean names who may activate the plan, each
  critical process's recovery objectives and manual workaround, the activation sequence, and how the plan is
  exercised and kept current. Full adds a disaster recovery section, structured on California's SIMM 5325-A;
  lean points to a separate disaster recovery plan instead.
- **Specs for `trade-study`, `use-case-specification` and `data-dictionary`**, each placed in a family by
  the spec or by a decision record. A trade study compares technical approaches against weighted criteria. A
  use case specification sets out one actor's numbered path to one goal. A data dictionary says what the
  fields of a dataset or table mean.

## Improved

- **Every quotation in the two new research logs was checked against the source's raw text**: 345 for the
  North Star document and 296 for the business continuity plan.
- **Two catalog entries were corrected.** The North Star entry gains its methodology and drops "North Star
  framework" as an alias, because that names the method rather than the document. The business continuity
  entry drops "RPO/RTO doc" and "ITIL/SOC2", which no source read supports. The runbook and the production
  readiness review entries now name the business continuity plan.
- **Two decision records widen family contracts to admit the next builds.** The `delivery-docs` contract
  admits a document that elaborates one actor's path, which admits the use case specification. The
  `governance-docs` contract admits the data dictionary as its seventh member, scoped to what fields mean.

## Fixed

- **Five family contracts described their own templates wrongly.** Each said the "How to fill this in"
  preamble opens each variant, yet all 57 of their variants open with frontmatter and carry the preamble
  inside the first comment block. The wording now matches the files. No obligation changed.
- **STATE and the README said the MCP server could fetch 85 template variants.** It serves 89, and its
  self-test asserts 89. Both now say 89.
- **The README said ten of its bundles were Tier-2 types.** Nineteen are. The README now gives the right
  number and points to STATE for the full list.

## Known issues

- **Nothing here makes any bundle better.** All 44 are `beta`, and no page calls one proven.
- **One per-feature document is declined, not built.** A measurement plan for a single feature does not
  ship, because its content already ships in the PRD
  ([ADR 0074](../internal/decisions/0074-analytics-requirements-measurement-plan-per-feature-is-declined.md)).
  A business-scope measurement plan has not been tested for admission.
- **One search can still not list a whole phase.** The MCP server's `search_templates` returns at most 8
  candidates, and the `deliver` phase holds 9. `total_matched` in the response tells you when a list was cut.
- **Build cost is measured, not a rate.** At API list rates, the business continuity plan cost $20.57 for the
  whole build. The North Star document cost at least $9.73; that figure is a floor, because part of its build
  was not captured ([`bundle-builds/`](../../bundle-builds/INDEX.md)).
