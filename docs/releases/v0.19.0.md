# product-lifecycle-templates v0.19.0

Release date: 2026-10-16. This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Summary

**The library grows from 45 bundles to 47 with a use-case specification and a trade study.** A use-case
specification spells out, for one user and one goal, the steps that succeed and the paths that branch off
them, so a team can build a story without guessing at its edge cases. A trade study compares named
technical options against weighted criteria and records which one the comparison favors, so the decision
that follows has a comparison to cite.

Neither new bundle is proven to help anyone. Both are researched, and each says where its sources disagree.

## New

- **[`use-case-specification`](../../templates/use-case-specification/)** - for one primary actor and one
  goal, a numbered main success scenario plus the alternative and exception paths that branch off it. Lean
  carries the title and actor, the goal in context, the preconditions and trigger, the main success
  scenario with one line of prose for each known deviation, and the minimal and success guarantees. Full
  adds scope and level, stakeholders and interests, extensions, related information, and open issues and
  schedule. The guide gives Alistair Cockburn's names beside the sizes: lean is his casual form in
  coverage, and full is his fully dressed form. It is the tenth `delivery-docs` member, and it elaborates
  one user story that the delivery chain already names.
- **[`trade-study`](../../templates/trade-study/)** - compares a named set of technical alternatives
  against weighted criteria, including a do-nothing baseline, and records the recommendation that the
  comparison supports. The decision itself is recorded elsewhere, normally in an ADR. Lean carries the
  problem and decision framing, the criteria and their weights, the alternatives, the scoring matrix and
  the recommendation. Full adds a sensitivity analysis, risks and benefits, dissent or independent review,
  and references. In its worked example, a four-point move in one weight erases the recommendation's
  lead. It is the fifth `decision-docs` member.

## Improved

- **Both agent skills now name the two new document types.** An agent decides whether to load a skill
  from its description, so a request to write or grade a use-case specification or a trade study now
  reaches the skill that fills or grades it.
- **Two existing guides now link to the new bundles.** The user stories guide already sent a reader who
  needs every actor-system flow written out to a use-case specification. The business case guide already
  sent a reader who is comparing technical approaches, rather than deciding whether to invest at all, to a
  trade study. Both mentions now link to the bundle.
- **Every quotation in both research logs was checked against the source's raw text**: 238 quotations
  from 27 sources for the use-case specification, and 263 quotations from 31 sources for the trade study.
- **Two catalog entries now describe what the library ships.** The use-case specification's entry no
  longer ties the type to Waterfall, UP or RUP, and it names the user story as its relationship. The trade
  study's entry names the RFC and the software design document as relationships, and it cites two
  verified sources in place of a BABOK section that the library had not read.

## Fixed

- **The gate now checks every citation in a companion, including one with a subsection named
  References.** It used to stop reading at the first place the text `## References` appeared, and a
  third-level heading such as `### References` contains that text. A broken citation after such a heading
  would have passed. The trade study's companion is the only one with that heading, and the fix shipped
  with it, so no released companion went unchecked.

## Known issues

- **Nothing here makes any bundle better.** All 47 are `beta`, and no page calls one proven.
- **A search filtered to the `develop` phase is now cut short too.** The MCP server's `search_templates`
  returns at most 8 candidates. The trade study brings `develop` to 9 bundles, beside `deliver` at 10, so
  a search filtered to either phase cannot list all of it. `total_matched` in the response tells you when
  a list was cut.
- **The trade study's worked example describes real products as they stood when it was researched.** Its
  facts about self-managed Kafka, Amazon MSK and RabbitMQ Streams come from vendor documentation read on
  2026-10-15, and that documentation changes. Check those facts before you treat the example as a current
  product comparison.
- **Build cost is measured, not a rate.** At API list rates, the whole build cost $16.74 across 15 agents
  for the use-case specification, and $20.96 across 16 agents for the trade study
  ([`bundle-builds/`](../../bundle-builds/INDEX.md)).
