## What this is not

This library does not claim its templates help anyone. Its gate checks structure and
research integrity - that every claim has a logged source, that no section drifts from
its family contract, that no count in the prose disagrees with the tree. **That is not
the same as evidence that a document produced from one of these templates is better than
one produced without it,** and no page here will say otherwise.
